For four years, I owned the platform foundation of an industrial IoT company. I joined when infrastructure changes were still applied by hand. By the end, a few hundred engineers were shipping through systems built to make changes visible, reviewable, and reversible.

The work did not arrive as separate projects. Each layer unlocked the next. Reviewable changes made faster feedback safe. Automation turned maintenance into background work. Rebuilt foundations made self-service possible. And those foundations carried the newest chapter: secure AI tooling and customer-facing platforms.
